# Notebooks, English

A bilingual research sequence on finite circle rotations. The notebooks call the same deterministic engine used by the certificate pipeline; they keep finite observations separate from the irrationality hypothesis.

## Topics

| Topic | Scope | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|
| [01-getting-started](01-getting-started/) | Circular ordering and the insertion mechanism | No level 3: research context is treated in topic 02 |
| [02-arithmetic-and-boundaries](02-arithmetic-and-boundaries/) | Farey and continued-fraction scales, rational boundaries, allocation controls, and research context | [03-research-perspectives](02-arithmetic-and-boundaries/03-research-perspectives.ipynb) |
